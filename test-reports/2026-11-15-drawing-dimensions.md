# Drawing dimensions audit — 2026-11-16 (lane MZ, drawdims)

Driven in headless Chrome with real mouse / key events (smoke `rfMZ`) on inch parts: a 14 ga bracket 6 x 3 with an UP
flange, two Ø.201 holes, a .375 slot and a #10 countersunk Hole Wizard hole; a 3/8 plate with a plain Ø.201, four
tapped 1/4-20 UNC, a counterbored 1/4 and a countersunk 1/4 Hole Wizard hole; a 16 ga Lofted-Bend square-to-round; a
perforated plate at 1:8. Compared step by step with SolidWorks 2024's behaviour. Engine checks in kernel `rfMZ`.
