## Fixed (my files)

**A1 / A2 / A3 (ui-commands.js `sketchThen`, `cmdExtrude`, `cmdSketchedBend`).** SolidWorks: with a planar face (or a
plane) picked first, Extruded Boss / Extruded Cut / Sketched Bend open a sketch on it and their PropertyManager comes
back at Exit Sketch; with nothing to use they ask for a plane or face. Anvil said "Create a sketch first" (Extrude) and
"Sketch the bend line(s) … first" (Sketched Bend) and stopped. `sketchThen` does what Base Flange/Tab already did
(`pendingAfterSketch`); a sketch selected in the FeatureManager or an unused sketch is still taken as before; an empty
sketch is discarded and nothing reopens. Smoke: job 1 knockouts, job 2 gusset, the Extruded Boss step.

**B1 refusal (ui-commands.js `bendSourceWhy`, Mirror PM).** The Mirror of an Edge-Flange / Hem / Jog / Sketched Bend on
a sheet-metal part is refused with the reason in the greyed ✓ and the status bar, and the way to get the same part
(the flange on both edges in one Edit Feature, which also keeps both equal). The flat was silently missing the mirrored
flange. Lift it when the kernel fix below lands. Smoke: job 2 Mirror.

**C1 (assembly.js `fitCylinder`).** The residual check proposed in audit 4 (B1 there): a patch set not tangent to the
fitted cylinder (within 1e-3 of the radius) is no cylinder. The assembly kernel groups pass 134 / 134 with it; rfNW's
fallback branch for it is no longer taken. Smoke: job 5 mates (the lid's underside is next to its bends).

**C2 (ui-assembly.js explode drag hook).** With the Explode PM open, a press on a component put it in the step at once
(for a drag), and the click's pick then toggled it out again: a plain click never selected anything. The component now
joins the step when a drag starts; a click toggles it through the pick, as SolidWorks. Smoke: job 5 exploded view.

## Handed on

**B1 kernel (kernel-rebuild.js mirror case; chamferpat / smpanel lanes).** Repro: `node anvil/headless/anvil.js build`
of `p.units("in"); p.sheet(0.1196, 0.12, 0.44); p.sketch("Top").rect(-2, -5, 4, 5).end().baseFlange();
p.flange(1.5, [[2, 0.1196, 1]], { position: "inside", lengthRef: "outer" }); p.mirror("Right", { feature: 3 });`
(feature 3 = the flange): the flat DXF has 1 bend instead of 2 and is 5.2920 wide instead of 6.5839, and the volume is
4.0060 in³ against 3.9507 for the same part with the flange on both edges (the mirrored bend keeps the base's square
corner). Fix: in the mirror build, when the source is a
sheet-metal bend feature (`flange` incl. hem / miter, `jog`, `sketchedBend`) and the model has `sheet`, build a copy of
the source feature with each `edges[i].at` reflected across the plane (offset applied), `pickRefs` dropped, through the
same flange / jog / sketched-bend builder, so it lands in the body and in `flatPattern`'s bends; then remove
`bendSourceWhy` from the Mirror PM. A Linear / Circular Pattern of a flange is likely the same (not measured).

## Notes (not changed)

**N1 (sheetmetal).** A four-edge Edge-Flange (Material Inside, no relief) leaves its corners .0071 open (end face at
z .0669, the other flange's inside face at .0598); Closed Corner's default gap .010 is wider, so ✓ is greyed with
"already within the gap". A user has to type a smaller gap. I could not tell where .0071 comes from; SolidWorks' own
four-edge flange gap is worth checking against it.

**N2.** A double-click on an Edge-Flange shows its length only; SolidWorks also shows its angle.

**N3.** Gusset side developed 1.59535 (flange 1.5 Material Inside + closed hem .375): a hand count with the hem radius
T/4 and the flange kept 1.5 high gives 1.62721; I could not confirm SolidWorks' hem development here, so this is not
called a defect. Hem1 also folds outward (the part reaches x 2.1235 past the flange's outside face at 2).

**N4.** After File ▸ Save as LID-ASM the PDF is LID-ASM.pdf but `model.name` stays "Assem1".

**N5.** Mirror is found in the Linear Pattern flyout of the Features tab; SolidWorks 2024's Features tab also has it as
its own button (worth checking against the user's screenshots before moving it).
