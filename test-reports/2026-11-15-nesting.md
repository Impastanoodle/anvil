# Geometry Nesting audit — lane NB, 2026-11-15

Engine `anvil/nest.js`, command `anvil/ui-nesting.js` (Mastercam profile, Transform ▸ Geometry Nesting). Inches throughout.

## How it was checked

- **Sets**: (a) 40 rectangles 6 × 4; (b) 12 L brackets (10 × 8 with a corner out, a .5 notch, two Ø.5 holes) + 30 gussets (4 × 4 triangles) + 8 discs Ø5 + 6 Anvil flat patterns (inch bracket, one bend, Ø.5 hole, built by the kernel); (c) a 30 × 20 tray blank with 2.5 corner notches, two 6 × 4 windows and a Ø3 hole + 20 washers Ø1.5 / Ø.625; (d) 60 parts of 6 shapes, 10 each (20 × 12 plate with R1 corners and 4 holes, L 16, disc Ø10 with a Ø2 hole, triangle 18 × 12, C half-ring R5 / R3, 14 × 4 slot with two holes); (e) a 40 × 30 panel with 600 holes Ø.5 + 30 tabs 3 × 2; (f) 24 C half-rings (concave, true arcs).
- **Options**: sheets 96 × 48 and 120 × 48; spacing / margin .125 / .25 and .25 / .5; rotation free (15° steps), 90° steps, 180° only (grain), none. 96 nests, Nest in holes on.
- **Checker** (`rfNBExact`, also in the audit script): distances between the true lines and arcs (line–line, line–arc, arc–arc incl. circle–circle intersection, no polygons), material containment, the margin on the exact extents, each copy placed or unplaced exactly once, and a part inside another's outer loop refused when Nest in holes is off. Determinism: every nest run twice, placements compared.
- **Baselines**: the area lower bound (part area / usable sheet area, rounded up) and first-fit-decreasing shelf packing of the bounding boxes (turned to lie flat when rotation allows).

## Results (after the fixes)

Every one of the 96 nests passed the exact check, gave the same placements twice, and never needed more sheets than shelf packing. Before the fixes the results were the same except for one defect: in set (c) no washer went into the tray's windows or hole (inH 0), see *Defects*.

### Reading the table

- **Sheets**: set (d) on 96 × 48 takes 2 sheets where shelf packing takes 3 (at every rotation but none); with no rotation it takes 3 (the third holds one part, 2.3 %) against an area bound of 2. On 120 × 48 it takes 2, as shelf packing does. Every other set fits one sheet, as the bound says.
- **Gaps**: the nest keeps the spacing everywhere, and runs over it by 0.003–0.07 in (.256–.32 for .25 spacing, .128–.20 for .125; .36 for the C-rings on 120 × 48 with 180° / no rotation). That is the raster: cells of sheet / 2000 (.048 in on 96, .06 on 120), each part's clearance band marked conservatively on its own grid. Accuracy ▸ Fine (sheet / 3000) halves it at about twice the time. Left as it is (see *Left*).
- **Time**: set (d) at 15° steps is the slowest job: 136 orientations rasterised once (prep ~1–1.7 s) and five placing strategies of ~0.7–1.4 s each (`result.timing`). 90° steps: about 1 s. Set (e), the 600-hole panel, 2–4 s at 15°, ~0.3–0.5 s at 90°.
- **Part in part**: set (c) puts 11 (spacing .25) or 13 (spacing .125) of the 20 washers in the tray's two windows and its Ø3 hole.

## Output

- Sheet DXF: LINE / ARC / CIRCLE through `Kernel.exportDXFSketch` (true arcs and circles, never chords; checked: set C-rings write 2 ARCs + 2 LINEs per part), inches ($INSUNITS 1), R12 or R2000; cuts on CUT (or OUTLINE / HOLES), bend lines on BEND (ACI 3), etch on ETCH, forms on FORM, sheet outline on SHEET (ACI 8). `headless/dxf-check.js` clean on every file the rows write.
- **Bend / etch lines in DXF is off by default in the PM** because smoke rfBM pins Save DXF to CUT-only entities; the engine (`Nest.sheetDXF`) writes them by default. The head may want it on by default (then rfBM's `every(e => e.layer === 'CUT')` needs the bend layer excluded).
- The Nest document (✓) keeps the marks in its sheet sketches with the BEND / ETCH layers.

## Left

- Gaps over the spacing (raster), see above. A real fix is an exact compaction pass (slide each part toward the start corner by the exact curve distance), which would not change the sheet count of these sets; not done.
- Undo inside the PM: Ctrl+Z cancels the PM (lane BY rule); the Nest document made by ✓ is a new document, so there is nothing to undo into. Not changed.
- The quick part-in-part rule only tries holes at least the smallest part's unrotated short side plus twice the spacing wide; a long thin part that would fit a narrower hole only when turned is still found by the normal bottom-left search when the hole is reached.
