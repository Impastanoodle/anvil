## Defects and notes

**C1 (fixed, ui-commands.js `cmdInsertBendsPM`).** A customer part whose bends are all rounded (R .06): Insert Bends
keeps them at their own radius, but the Bend radius field stayed at the generic default .0625 and became the sheet's
default, so the Edge-Flange added next bent at R .0625 and the blank came out 6.2909 instead of 6.2920 (hand: 4 + 1.5 −
BD + 1 − BD). The PM now takes the part's radius as it takes the part's thickness: when every bend found is already
rounded and they share one radius, Bend radius is set to it until the user types one. Smoke rfNN job c pins the sheet's
radius (.06) and the blank to 1e-4.

**F1 (fixed, ui-commands.js `faceChildren` / `deleteFeature`).** Delete Flange A said only "Delete Flange A?"; Flange B,
built on Flange A's edge, was not listed and turned up dangling afterwards. SolidWorks' Confirm Delete lists such
children with *Delete child features* unchecked. `faceChildren` now counts a feature whose `pickRefs` (`on` / `fids`)
name a doomed feature, and the dialog reads "Built on its faces or edges: Flange B" with *Also delete these child
features* unchecked (left unchecked, B stays and dangles, as before). Smoke rfNN job f asserts it; it fails on main.

**F2 (handed on: the FeatureManager, ui-panels.js / the tree).** There is no way to reorder features: no drag in the
FeatureManager, no menu row, no API. SolidWorks drags a feature to a new place in the tree (refusing above its parents).
Proposal: drag of a `.ti[data-fid]` row with a drop line between rows; the drop calls a kernel `moveFeature(model, id,
beforeId)` that refuses when the move puts the feature above anything it uses (`sketch`, `feature`, `profile`, `path`,
`plane.featureId`, `plane.face.feature`, `pickRefs` `on` / `fids`) or below anything that uses it, with the reason in the
status bar; one undo step; then a rebuild.

**E1 (handed on: ui-nesting.js `addFlat`).** Both configurations' flats join the nest as "FAM-1": the parts list, the
report and the sheet labels cannot tell them apart. Proposal: when the model has more than one configuration, name the
part `m.name + ' [' + K.activeConfiguration(m).name + ']'` (SolidWorks' cut list names the configuration).

**A1 (handed on: lane NJ, Closed Corner PM OK greying).** Corners ▸ Closed Corner, ✓ with no end face picked: the PM
closes and nothing is added (no message). Proposal: `okBlocked` "Pick the end faces of the flanges to close." as the
other PMs this round.

**A2 (handed on: ui-commands.js `defaultSource`, low).** Linear Pattern opened right after another command takes the
feature still selected in the tree (here the Corner Relief made just before) as *Features to Pattern*; patterning a
Corner Relief silently made four .024 in² slivers in the bends. SolidWorks opens with the list empty unless the user
preselected. Proposal: only a selection the user made (not the auto-selected new feature), else the last cut / boss.

**A3 (note, drawing lane).** Autodimension on the box's flat gives two ordinate chains as expected, but values .015
apart (1.948 / 1.963 and 9.829 / 9.844: a bend line and the corner-relief edge next to it) print on top of each other.
Proposal: in `flatAutodimension`, drop the relief edges or merge ordinates closer than a text height.

**D1 (note).** A Hole Table on the 600-hole flat is built and warns "600 rows do not fit on the sheet. Use a hole
callout on the pattern (600X), Combine same sizes, or a larger sheet." Right; SolidWorks also needs Combine same sizes.

**D2 (note).** A Hole Table origin must be a vertex: the bounding-box corner of a blank with open corners (four
flanges, a cross) is refused with a clear message. As SolidWorks.

**Note (b).** The DXF/DWG Import PM's Units default "file" read the inch header correctly; the imported sketch is on Top.

**Note (e).** A sketch dimension written as `rect(0, -3, "=W", 3)` in a script is evaluated once (README: sketch
entities hold no expressions), so a configuration does not change it; the family was driven through the flange length.

## Follow-up (lane NN2, on local main with lane NJ's ✓ greying)

**A1 (already right on merged main; pinned).** With nothing picked, ✓ is greyed with the reason and a click adds
nothing: Closed Corner "Select at least one face to extend.", Corner Relief "Select at least one corner (or Collect all
corners).", Break-Corner "Select at least one corner edge or face.", Welded Corner "Select a side face of at least one
corner to weld.", Rip "Select at least one edge to rip.", Fold "The part is not unfolded: add an Unfold first.", Hem
"Select at least one edge."; Jog opens no PM ("select a flat face of the sheet and click Jog, or sketch one line on it
first"). Unfold is the exception by design: its boxes say "default: the base face" / "empty: every bend", so ✓ adds an
Unfold of every bend (SolidWorks needs a fixed face picked; left as is, noted). Smoke row rfNN "sheet-metal
PropertyManagers opened with nothing picked".

**A2 (fixed, ui-commands.js).** Linear Pattern, Circular Pattern and Mirror take only what the user selected before the
command (a feature in the FeatureManager or a face of it); the feature a ✓ selects afterwards is marked `auto` and no
longer counts, and with nothing selected Features to Pattern / Mirror shows "(select a feature)", ✓ is greyed with
"Select the feature to pattern: click a face of it, or pick it in the list.", no preview runs, and a click on a face in
the graphics area fills it. Existing test changed: r4-features4 "Linear Pattern spacing arrow" opened the PM right after
an `anvil.run` and relied on the old fallback (the last feature); it now clicks the boss in the FeatureManager first.
Smoke row rfNN "Linear Pattern / Circular Pattern / Mirror right after another feature's ✓".

**E1 (fixed, ui-nesting.js `addFlat`).** A part with more than one configuration joins the nest as "<part>
<configuration>" ("FAM-1 Default", "FAM-1 WIDE"); the parts list, the report and the sheet labels use that name.

**Timing note.** Job d's Make Drawing (600-hole panel) took 17.3 s on merged main against 3.7 to 5.2 s on 0.1.27 in
the first run (same machine, busier: seven agents); worth one timing alone. Job d is now two steps so each stays under
the runner's 30 s on a busy machine.
