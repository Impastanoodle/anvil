What passed. Job a: the customer file opens as an inch part, Insert Bends finds both rounded bends with their own
radius, the flat DXF is one outline, four holes and two bend lines at the blank's size; the same part written by
OpenCascade 7.6 (B-spline hole edges on the legs) reads to the same blank. The flat placed by a click takes the sheet
scale (1:2) as SolidWorks does; its Bend Table reads A / B, UP, 90°, 0.075, .130; Autodimension gives ordinates 0, .750,
1.435, 2.870, 4.305, 4.990, 5.740 across and 0, 1.500, 3.000, 4.500, 6.000 up (the leg hole .750 from the edge, the bend
lines at leg + BA / 2), all inside the border and clear of the title block. Job b: Closed Corner's three clicks (the
front pair share a plane) with SolidWorks' defaults, Collect all corners finds the four 2 Bend corners, the Welded
Corner after the Closed Corner adds metal and the flat leaves it out; the solid is closed; the drawing's DWG is AutoCAD
2000 (AC1015, 12.9 KB) and the PDF one page per sheet. Job c: both flat DXFs read back closed with their holes and bend
lines. No console errors; every step under 3 s.

## Findings, ranked by how much they would bite a shop

1. **Cut-List Properties linked in the title block printed empty (high). Fixed.** Repro: job b drawing, double-click
   FINISH, type `BLANK $PRPSHEET:"Bounding Box Length" X $PRPSHEET:"Bounding Box Width"`, Enter. Expected: the sheet,
   the DXF, the DWG and the PDF read `BLANK 13.7401 X 9.7401` (SolidWorks resolves cut-list links in the sheet format).
   Got: `BLANK  X` on the sheet and in every export. The edit box and `anvil.drawing.titleBlock()` showed the right
   value, so it looked right until printed. Cause: the sheet was worked out with the build only, and the cut list needs
   the mesh. Fix: drawing-render.js `computeSheet` hands its mesh to `titleBlock`, drawing-ui.js `cctx()` carries
   `App.mesh`. Tests: kernel rfKM "job b drawing" (sheet, DXF, PDF; fails on the old code), smoke rfKM job b drawing.
2. **A hole across a Lofted-Bend's seam is cut in the part but missing from the flat, with no warning (high, kernel:
   head).** Repro: the transition (c1) + `p.sketch("Front").circle(0, 1, 0.25).end().extrude(4, { cut: true });`
   (Ø.5 at x 0, through the side the seam is on). The part loses .01568 in³, the flat stays one loop with no warning,
   so the laser blank has no hole. Expected: the hole laid as two half notches at the blank's two seam edges (SolidWorks
   lays it), or at least a warning. Holes elsewhere on it (four places tried) are laid. kernel-sheetmetal.js Lofted-Bend
   flat (the mid-surface slice stops at the seam: README says a notch from the blank's edge is not laid yet). TODO row
   in kernel rfKM.
3. **Closed Corner (bend region closed) + Circular Corner Relief leaves two specks in the flat (medium, kernel: head).**
   Repro: job b without the weld (kernel rfKM `BOX + RELIEF`). The flat has two extra loops of 5.3e-5 in² (about .008 x
   .013 in) at the back corners, the ones where a single wall's end face was picked; the DXF carries them on HOLES, so
   the laser pierces two specks. Rectangular reliefs, or Open bend region on, leave none. Expected: no sliver loops.
   kernel-sheetmetal.js Corner Relief cut on a closed bend region. TODO row in kernel rfKM.
4. **Autodimension on a flat view could run ordinates across the border frame (medium). Fixed.** Repro: a flat view
   placed near the sheet's edge, Tools ▸ Dimensions ▸ Autodimension… ✓. Expected: dimensions inside the border, the
   view slid or the other side used when there is no room. Got: the room check allowed 5 mm from the paper edge (the
   border is at 10 mm), and the first / last values, centred on the view's edges, stuck out past the strip it checked;
   at the corners no slide was tried along the strip. Several of 24 placements of job a's flat near the edges put values across the border (and with the flat fitted by the script, job a's own sheet did too). Fix: drawing-annotations.js
   `flatAutodimension`: margin = border + 1 mm, the strip runs a text height past each end, a second pass slides the
   view along the strip. Tests: kernel rfKM (24 placements near all four edges; fails on the old code), smoke rfKM job a.
5. **Make Drawing from Part leaves no room for the flat on Sheet1 (low).** Repro: job b, Make Drawing from Part on ANSI
   B: Standard 3 View + Isometric at 1:2, then Insert ▸ Drawing View ▸ Flat Pattern…: the flat at sheet scale (6.87 x
   4.87 in) fits nowhere clear on Sheet1, so the test adds Sheet2 (the sheet tab's Add Sheet) and places it there. A
   shop usually wants the flat on its own sheet anyway; SolidWorks' Make Drawing places only the views you drag from the
   View Palette. No change.
6. **Butt Closed Corner makes the blank smaller (note).** Job b's blank loses .130 in² over the four corners: Butt stops
   both walls gap/√2 short of the other's inside face, so the front and back walls lose their corner ends. That is
   SolidWorks 2022 as measured (rfDU); a shop that wants one wall lapping the other picks Overlap. No change.
7. **Lofted-Bend Bend Table (note).** The transition's flat has 20 bend lines (R 0, 13°–51°); a Bend Table on it is 20
   rows with Inner Radius 0 and Bend Ded. "-". Not compared with SolidWorks (not opened).
8. **Swept Flange profile (note).** The profile line is the inside face (T on its right as drawn): the channel's 1 / 2 /
   1 legs are inside dimensions. As SolidWorks' open-profile base flange; Reverse direction flips it.
