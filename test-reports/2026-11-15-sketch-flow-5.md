# Sketch flow audit, round 5 (lane sketchflow5, rfNK)

All flows were driven with real mouse and key events in headless Chrome, on inch parts (Tools ▸ Default units ▸ Inches, then File ▸ New). After each flow the run edited dimensions, undid every step, redid them all, exited the sketch, edited it again and built the feature. These flows are not the ones covered in rounds 1–4. The tests are in `anvil/tests/smoke/rfNK.js` (8 steps) and `anvil/tests/kernel/rfNK.js` (4 tests).

| # | Flow | Steps driven | Defects found | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | The four slot tools | Straight Slot (3 clicks). Width dimensioned from the two sides, typed 1/2. Centre distance from the two arc centres, typed 3 1/2. Centerpoint Straight Slot with *Add dimensions* and *Overall Length* checked in its PM (adds Overall 2.750 and width .750). 3 Point Arc Slot and Centerpoint Arc Slot (each adds its R). Width changed .500 → .625 from its label. End arc dragged: the slot moves and keeps its length and width. Undo / redo 8 steps, edit again, Boss-Extrude | none. | g | Dimensions to arcs | Line to circle: centre (2). **Shift+click** on the circle's far side, typed 2 3/4. Shift+click on its near side (Min). Radius on an arc, then right-click ▸ Properties… ▸ Display: Diameter (Ø). Arc length: the arc, then its two ends, typed 2. Chord between the two ends: radius and arc length already fix it, so Make Dimension Driven? appears and the chord is made driven. Undo / redo, Boss-Extrude | **Shift+click did nothing**: Min / Max were reachable only through the Dimension PM's arc conditions. | **fixed** (`ui-sketch.js` `dimToolClick` + `shiftArcConds`: a Shift+click on a circle or arc records where it was clicked. The side facing the other entity is Min and the far side is Max, stored as the relation's `ac`) |
| h | 400 holes | 48 × 24 plate. A 1/4 hole box-selected. Linear Sketch Pattern 20 × 2.4 by 20 × 1.15 with Dimension Y spacing (400 holes). Zoom out and box-select all (404 entities). Drag the seed. Spacing label 2.4 → 2.25. Window-select and Delete 50 holes. Undo / redo. Each action counted in solver calls, analyses and redraws | (1) **Every redraw re-analysed the whole sketch**: about 1.3–5 s each on this sketch, so a box select, a zoom or a click on empty space waited as long as an edit. (2) **A seed drag took about 4 s per mouse move** and a spacing edit took longer than the 30 s smoke step limit: every solve carried the 1200 unknowns of the copies through dense matrices. (3) **Deleting 50 holes took over 30 s**: one redraw (and analysis) per deleted entity | **fixed**: (1) `ui-core.js` `cachedAnalysis` keeps the analysis against the sketch's content, so a box select and a zoom now run 0 analyses. (2) `sketch-solver.js` `condensePatterns` is used by `solve` and `drag`. A copy named only by its Pattern relation (no other relation or dimension, not an anchor, not the dragged entity) is left out of the unknowns and placed afterwards by the relation's `place()`. The result is identical to the full solve (kernel rfNK checks the geometry to 1e-7). A drag is now 3 drag solves and 1 analysis; the whole flow up to the spacing edit takes about 4 s. (3) `ui-select.js` `deleteSelection0` redraws once for a multi-delete: 1 analysis, about 0.8 s. **Left**: `analyze` (the rank test) still costs about 1.3 s once per real change on 400 holes; copies that carry a dimension are not condensed. |

Not checked on a SolidWorks seat: the Min / Max side rule for Shift+click (taken from the help text: "the side you click"), and whether SolidWorks' Slot PM changes a placed slot's length type.
