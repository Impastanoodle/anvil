## Ranked mismatches (what a shop would notice)

1. **Flange below 90°, Material Inside (wrong flat).** Off on all 38 rows at 30° and 45°: 0.002 to 0.25 in, volume up to
   9 %. SolidWorks' rule at every angle: the outer virtual sharp sits `s = −T·cot(A)` past the base edge; the flange's outer
   face runs through the base's top edge. Anvil uses `(R+T)(tan(A/2) − 1)` below 90°. Hand-checked at 16 ga 45°, R = T and
   R = 3T, and 30°. `flangeLayout`.
2. **Flange, Material Outside (wrong flat).** Rule `s = T·tan(A/2)`; Anvil uses `(R+T)(tan(A/2) − 1) + T`. Off by 0.035 to
   0.085 in at 30°, 45°, 120°, 135° on 16 ga; matches at 90°. `flangeLayout`.
3. **Jog at 120° (wrong solid, no error).** 16 ga: Anvil's bbox reaches x = −3.2 with volume 0.4335; SolidWorks 0.4784, x
   from 0 to 3.38. `buildSketchJog`.
4. **Multi-edge flange, mitred corners (M03, M24).** SolidWorks' auto miter is really Butt with the bend closed, gap 0.01.
   Anvil makes a 45° mitre at gap 0: solid 0.7 % heavy, flat area 8e-4 heavy. OpenCascade agreed only because it gets the
   same layout. `flangeLayout`.
5. **Rip "both".** SolidWorks cuts walls square, each `T + g/√2` from the outer corner; Anvil cuts a 45° slit. +2.3 %
   (0.668093 vs 0.652974). One side +6.4e-4. `ripLayout`.
6. **Miter Flange flat.** No 45° line: a square notch at `T + g/√2`, same rule as 5. Solid +4.5e-3 (0.818948 vs 0.815259),
   blank 0.815091 vs 0.815429. Also G38. `miterFlangeStages`. Anvil builds gap 0; SolidWorks refuses it.
7. **Closed bend fill.** Flats match to six decimals, including 20 ga R 0.0718. The fill is two ruled faces tapering from
   the bend's start line to the wall end, not a prism: +6.1e-4. `closedCornerFill`.
8. **3+ section lofts.** See section 4.
9. **Jog at K ≠ 0.5, Overall Dimension.** 11 ga K 0.71: 0.939273 vs 0.937926, geometry equal. `buildSketchJog`.
10. **Small.** G46 Overlap ratio 0.5 (+1.7e-4). G47 four Underlap corners (−1.4e-3). M18 and M26 hole walls (+2.5e-5), known.
